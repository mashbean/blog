Borderless Nomadism: How Digital Drifters Try to Put Down Roots
Yen-Lin Huang · Nowhere Taiwan Season · London · 20 September 2026

Field observations remain attributed to the speaker. Product documentation establishes the stated technical mechanisms but does not establish social outcomes. Literary works are used as comparisons rather than predictions.

Yen-Lin Huang | Chiang Mai field notes and three experiments in crypto-community settlement

Two essays supplied by the speaker. Pop-up city, guerrilla experimentation and new society are the speaker's working categories. The Chiang Mai account describes a particular field visit and does not represent every digital nomad.

Yen-Lin Huang | Talk outline and orally presented cases

The overseas WeChat user, Boston driver, daughter from Yunlin, SCP, Haitang and museum volunteer remain case prompts from the supplied outline. No unprovided biography, dialogue or identity has been invented.

From Household Registers to Exile: Identity Autonomy in Digital Asia

Yen-Lin Huang, 2025. Used for the division of institutional roles, the dual identity management of states and platforms, and three central questions. Policy dates in the essay are not presented as the latest 2026 status.

The Internet Does Not Merely Return 404s: It Decays Faster Than We Think

Yen-Lin Huang, ARTouch, 2 July 2024. Used for data loss, maintenance costs and autonomous networks; the image is a locally preserved original from the author's site.

Revisiting Elixus after Twenty Years: A Tribal Aesthetic Born Online

Yen-Lin Huang, ARTouch, 2 June 2023. Covers Elixus across IRC, blogs and tea-house meetings, and the 2001 Piraport and PiraGene identity and copyright experiments.

An Escape Room about Social Platforms

Yen-Lin Huang, 2026. The five exit routes and the distinctions among protocol, operations and governance come from slides 21–27 of the earlier deck; no long passage from the book is reproduced.

Regional Revitalisation and Digital Villagers: Visiting Yamakoshi and Its DAO

Chang Pao-Cheng and Yen-Lin Huang, ARTouch, 29 March 2024. Snow photograph by Chang; NFT image from Chang's collection, work by ykxotkx; group image credited in the article to Nishikigoi NFT Discord; governance screen from Nishikigoi NFT Snapshot. A half-day visit does not establish long-term demographic or economic effects.

Philip Zimmermann | Preface to PGP Source Code and Internals

MIT Press, 1995. The book published C source code, not users' private keys. The slide focuses on the tension between print publication and controls on exporting cryptographic software.

John Perry Barlow | A Declaration of the Independence of Cyberspace

Original dated 8 February 1996 and preserved by EFF. The deck quotes a short passage and frames the remaining text as the speaker's question to this historical declaration.

Cat Thu | Notes from Zuzalu

A resident account of shared life, classes, meals and discussion at Zuzalu in Montenegro in April and May 2023. The Luštica Bay moonrise and ZK class photographs are by Cat Thu.

WIRED | Amir Taaki's Work in Rojava

Andy Greenberg, 29 March 2017. Reports about fifteen months in the Middle East; after leaving the front, Taaki taught open-source software and internet use, worked on a fertiliser factory and solar research, and helped design a technical curriculum. Portrait by Anastasia Taylor-Lind for WIRED.

CNA | Reporting from Network School in Forest City

29 July 2026. The slide uses CNA's 16 July 2026 photograph of the Network School display space by Zamzahuri Abas. The image documents a physical site and does not endorse its politics or operating claims.

Rarimo | Freedom Tool Documentation

Describes passport verification, zero-knowledge proofs and blockchain voting components, and lists Russia2024, Iranians Vote and United Space. No direct source was found for a 'Rarimo Catalonia vote', so that outline prompt is marked as unverified.

Government of Catalonia | Vocdoni Digital Voting Case

The Government of Catalonia lists Vocdoni as a blockchain success case and describes a universally verifiable, decentralised, anonymous and censorship-resistant voting infrastructure. It is separate from Rarimo and is not presented as a recognised national election.

Deutsches Museum | Amateur Radio / DLØDM

The museum confirms DLØDM amateur-radio demonstrations in cooperation with DARC. The elderly volunteer is the speaker's personal observation; no name is inferred.

Deutsches Museum | DLØDM Amateur Radio Licence

Museum article from 2024. The slide uses a June 2022 station photograph credited to Deutsches Museum, Munich; it is not a photograph of the speaker's visit.

Isaac Asimov | The Naked Sun (1957)

Solaria's sparse population, robot labour and mediated social contact are used as a literary comparison, not a prediction or a statistic about real society.

Owl Publishing | Traditional Chinese Edition of The Naked Sun

Traditional Chinese edition published in 2013, translated by Yeh Lee-hwa, ISBN 9789862621530. The deck shows the English and Taiwan Traditional Chinese covers together.

The Naked Sun | Plot and Textual Context for Solaria

Used to check the work and its plot. The slide avoids population figures from later portrayals of Solaria in Foundation and Earth.

Ted Kaczynski | Industrial Society and Its Future, paragraph 173

Original text from 1995, paragraph 173. A short quotation is used for critical reading; the deck rejects the author's violence.

AI, Work and Agency | Cultural Frontline II Reading Group

Yen-Lin Huang. Preserves the disagreements among Susskind, Srnicek and Williams, and Acemoglu, Autor and Johnson. The authors' assumptions are not treated as completed empirical outcomes.
