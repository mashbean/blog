【J-AMSA投稿】
J-AMSA(Journal of Asian Medical Students’ Association)是國際性的，同儕評審(peer-reviewed)的期刊，目前被收錄於BASE, Index Copernicus International, Google Scholar, Cengage Learning, ICMJE, 及Ulrichs web 等索引中。
目前J-AMSA接受
(1) original articles: basic medical research, clinical research, public health or health care management; 
(2) case report; (3) review article; (4) letter to editor; (5) book review;
(6) perspective article 六種形式之文章，與醫學相關之內容都接受，形式內容皆相當廣泛。不論是有進實驗室做專題的同學，或只是想發表自己對於特定主題的看法，都相當歡迎投稿。最佳之稿件會再額外頒獎。
請至
http://j-amsa.amsa-international.org/…/ma…/about/submissions
進行投稿
投稿期限目前已延長至Dec 19, 2014
